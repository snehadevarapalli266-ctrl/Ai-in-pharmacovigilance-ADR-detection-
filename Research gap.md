# Research Gap and Future Directions

## 1. Identified Research Gap

Existing studies have investigated artificial intelligence,
machine learning, and natural language processing for
adverse drug reaction detection.

However, questions remain about how consistently these
methods perform across different datasets and healthcare
settings.

## 2. Key Research Challenges

- Incomplete or inconsistent medical data.
- False-positive and false-negative predictions.
- Limited external validation.
- Difficulty interpreting model outputs.
- Privacy and data-governance requirements.
- Distinguishing drug-event associations from causation.

## 3. Proposed Research Direction

A future pilot study could evaluate an NLP-assisted method
using a suitable publicly available dataset with reference
annotations.

Its performance could be assessed using precision, recall,
and F1-score, alongside a review of common errors.

## 4. Expected Contribution

The proposed work could help clarify the limitations of
automated ADR detection and identify where human review
remains essential.

## 5. Important Limitation

This research gap is preliminary. It must be checked against
the original scientific articles before being presented as
a confirmed gap in the literature.
